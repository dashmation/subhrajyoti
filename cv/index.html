About Me

Quality Engineering Leader

I work at the intersection of quality engineering, automation, and real-world delivery pressure. Over the last 10+ years, I've moved from writing manual test cases to leading QA for large-scale telecom and digital platforms — most recently as QA Lead driving end-to-end quality for checkout journeys (Broadband + SIM) at BT Group.

I don't believe in QA as a gatekeeper. I believe in QA as an accelerator. Build automation early (in-sprint), focus on high-signal coverage, reduce manual effort using AI and smart tooling, and keep teams practical — ship fast, but don't break the product.

Beyond tooling, I spend significant time mentoring engineers — not just on tools, but on how to think: what to automate, what not to, and how to avoid over-engineering.
